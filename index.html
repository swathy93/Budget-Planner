Connect your Google Sheet

This planner saves your monthly budget data to a Google Sheet so it persists across devices and refreshes.

  1. Open your Google Sheet and go to Extensions → Apps Script
  2. Paste the contents of Code.gs into the editor and save
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment, choose Web App, set Execute as Me and access to Anyone
  4. Copy the Web App URL and paste it below
